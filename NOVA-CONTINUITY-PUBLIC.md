# Nova Forge — Public Continuity Principles

Nova Forge is designed so the public site is portable and does not depend on one hosting provider for survival.

- The public site must remain deployable on standards-compliant static/web hosting.
- Provider-specific services should be replaceable through adapters or configuration.
- Critical catalogue identities, versions, hashes and provenance must remain exportable.
- Conversations and community metadata must have provider-neutral export/restore paths before they are treated as durable.
- Large mods, images and videos may use authorized external/free sources; Nova Forge should retain identity, metadata, verification and source/mirror references.
- A single disappearing host must not erase the catalogue record or project identity.
- Free quotas must degrade safely rather than trigger automatic paid overage.
- Read-only availability is preferable to data loss.
- Backups are only valid when a restore path exists.

Intended production domain once registered and activated: `getnovaforge.com`.
