# MODARYX MODS — VF Premium HD Design Contract

Status: locked visual direction recovered from the five Wolf + Dragon concept boards supplied by the founder on 2026-09-11.

## Brand migration rule

All visual language from the recovered Nova Forge concept boards is retained as art direction, but the public identity becomes MODARYX MODS and the OS identity becomes Modaryx OS. The old Nova Forge wordmark is not part of the final public design.

## Global visual language

- Cinematic fantasy-tech atmosphere: deep graphite / near-black base, forged gold / amber highlights, controlled electric blue accents, warm firelight, monumental depth.
- Signature companions: wolf + dragon as paired guardians/companions and emotional anchors of the ecosystem.
- Monumental luminous kingdom / citadel imagery, mountains, waterfalls, forge light, dark foreground framing.
- Premium serif display typography for large emotional headlines, restrained sans-serif for UI/navigation/body.
- Fine gold dividers, thin borders, subtle inner glows, glass/dark-metal panels, luminous but never gaudy CTAs.
- Visual density may be rich, but hierarchy must remain clean and readable.
- Motion must be deliberate and restrained; reduced-motion must preserve the complete experience.

## Placement contract — public website homepage

The homepage keeps the composition shown by the recovered `02` concept:

1. Top navigation bar
   - MODARYX mark + MODARYX MODS at far left.
   - Main navigation centered.
   - Search, account/sign-in and primary CTA grouped at the right.
2. Cinematic hero
   - Main headline / supporting copy / CTAs on the left.
   - Wolf + dragon + luminous kingdom as the central/right visual focus.
   - Short manifesto / signature phrase in the far-right visual column where space allows.
3. Primary ecosystem strip immediately below the hero
   - Five large destination cards preserving the recovered placement rhythm.
   - Final destinations map to the current product architecture: Games/Mods (Avalon), Creator Studio, Music, Hardware, Security/Trust.
4. Companion-growth / progression band below the ecosystem cards
   - Four stages inspired by the recovered companion board: Bébé → Jeune → Forgé → Royal.
   - This is narrative/brand progression, not a misleading product-status claim.
5. Closing vision / community CTA block.
6. Premium footer with compact ecosystem links and trust/legal surfaces.

## Recovered companion concept — board 01

The wolf and dragon are not decorative mascots only. Their relationship carries the brand story:

- Bébé: naissance du lien.
- Jeunes: exploration / apprentissage.
- Forgés: épreuves / force / protection.
- Royaux: alliance / création / héritage.

Use this progression for brand storytelling, onboarding moments, achievements, seasonal editorial visuals and community identity. Do not make it mandatory for basic site use.

## Recovered universe portals — board 03

The portal composition becomes the visual model for the ecosystem/game discovery surface:

- One dominant central portal / destination.
- Secondary portals around it, each with a clear color identity and category.
- Large cards below for direct access.
- Search remains prominent at the top.
- The system must scale beyond the original categories to game hubs such as GTA 6, Red Dead Redemption 2 and future titles.

For SEO and usability, portals are presentation; each destination must also resolve to a real crawlable page/hub.

## Modaryx OS Public — board 04

Preserve the recovered placement logic:

- Fixed/anchored vertical navigation rail on the left.
- Product title and edition identity at the upper-left content area.
- Global search / notifications / account controls at the top-right.
- Large cinematic home hero occupying the upper center/right.
- Four major task cards beneath: Play, Explore, Create, Equip.
- Recent activity and progression/status panels below.
- Modaryx Guide as a distinct assistant surface at the lower-right.

Public edition must remain calm, readable and fail-closed. Decorative richness must never obscure state, security or provenance.

## Modaryx OS Founder — board 05

Preserve the same family resemblance but make Founder visibly more ceremonial/premium:

- Left vertical rail remains the main navigation anchor.
- Founder identity appears in the top frame, never as a random badge.
- Hero places wolf + dragon and the kingdom as the central prestige image.
- Four major action cards remain aligned beneath the hero.
- Founder-only surfaces such as Legacy / Library / Vault may appear in the left rail only when the feature exists.
- Founder visual treatment may use richer gold, heraldic frames and legacy motifs, but must not imply unavailable privileges/features.

Public green is never assumed from Founder green, and Founder green is never assumed from Public green.

## Website information architecture to integrate before VF

- `/games/` master multigaming hub.
- `/gta-6/mods/` flagship GTA 6 mods hub.
- `/red-dead-redemption-2/mods/` RDR2 mods hub.
- Game-specific categories, guides and individual mod pages.
- Catalogue/search surfaces wired to real crawlable destinations rather than empty SEO placeholders.
- Creator Studio, profiles/community, provenance/security, Modaryx Guide, downloads/documentation and Modaryx OS surfaces harmonized to this same visual family.

## Quality bar

The target is not a cosmetic reskin. The VF must feel like one authored premium ecosystem across homepage, hubs, tools and OS surfaces. Layout rhythm from the recovered boards is treated as intentional and should not be casually rearranged. Changes to placement require a concrete usability, accessibility, responsive or product reason.

## Safety / truthfulness

- Never display fake online-player counts, fake release states, fake compatibility, fake verification or fake security badges.
- Unknown remains unknown.
- Security/provenance language remains evidence-backed.
- No remote dependency is allowed to become critical to Modaryx OS local operation.
